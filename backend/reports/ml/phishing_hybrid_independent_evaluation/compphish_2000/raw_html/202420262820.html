Avatar Maker

Welcome to our avatar maker! This free online tool can help you create an avatar that looks just like you. There is no software to download and you can make as many avatars as you wish.

Why would you need a free avatar maker?

Lots of people don’t like social media. They feel that it isn’t private enough, too fake, or just don’t think it’s fun to use. However, those same people might still want to have a social media account if only to connect with friends and family.

Having an avatar that looks like you but isn’t really you might be ideal for people in this situation. It would allow them to have a social media presence but not have their default photo be an actual photo of themselves.

This avatar could be used across the entire web, not just on social media. You could use it for comments on blog posts, your default photo on web forums like Reddit, or even as the main picture for your Google account.

Free Avatar Maker: How does it work?

This tool is very easy to use and can be a lot of fun. First, you’ll need to select your gender. If you don’t feel you fall into a traditional male/female identity, pick the gender that you feel most closely aligns with yours. The only difference between the two systems is the options you’ll have to customize your avatar.

Once you select your gender, you’ll see the default avatar. At the top of the page, you’ll see four choices for the overall look of the avatar. The default is more realistic, while the second is more polygonal. The third is an anime-style avatar and the fourth looks like a black-and-white comic strip.

With your style selected, it’s time to customize your avatar so it looks just like you. There are a wide variety of options including facial shape, eye size and shape, eye color, hair style and color, glasses, and even clothing and background. Play around with all the tools so your avatar looks like you — or not like you! Feel free to be as creative as you like. The RANDOM button is a fun place to start if you’re not looking for an avatar that closely aligns with your own look.

While you’re making customization choices, each feature will have various modifiers that appear underneath the avatar itself. With these controls, you can make aspects larger or smaller, move them around, or change the color.

If you make a mistake with this avatar maker, use the UNDO and REDO buttons to travel back or forward in time. If you feel like you’ve made too many mistakes, the RESET button will bring you back to the beginning. Don’t worry: if you accidentally hit the RESET button, just hit the UNDO button to go back to where you left off.

How to download your avatar?

Once you are happy with how your avatar looks, you can hit the DOWNLOAD button. You have three choices for your download:

1. PNG (200px) — A small pixel-based image file with transparency support

2. PNG (400px) — A larger pixel-based image file with transparency support

3. SVG — A vector-based image that can be resized as large as needed

Do note that you’ll need an image editor that supports vector files to work with the SVG image, such as Adobe Illustrator.
