Help Desk

HELPDESK : 011-24041001
Candidates seeking clarification, guidance, or assistance related to the application process or examination details during filling up of application form can contact the helpline at 011-24041001. The helpline will be operational from 10:00 AM to 5:30 PM on all working days from notification of an examination up to the last date of submission. Applicants may utilize this service for any issues relating to the application process, including registration, fee payment, uploading of documents, etc.
