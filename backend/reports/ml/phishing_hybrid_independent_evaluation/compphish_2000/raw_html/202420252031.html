Treatment Categories at Pristyn Care

Proctology – Pristyn Care specializes in treating conditions such as piles, fissures, fistulas, and pilonidal sinus using advanced laser techniques. These treatments offer quick recovery, less pain, and minimal risk of complications.

Laparoscopic – Laparoscopic surgery at Pristyn Care involves small incisions and the use of a laparoscope to treat conditions like hernias, gallstones, and appendicitis. This approach reduces recovery time, minimizes pain, and lowers the risk of infection.

ENT (Ear, Nose, and Throat) – Pristyn Care offers comprehensive ENT treatments, including procedures for tonsillitis, sinusitis, deviated septum, and hearing loss. Using the latest technology, their ENT specialists ensure precise diagnosis and effective treatment.

Vascular – Pristyn Care provides advanced treatments for vascular conditions such as varicose veins and deep vein thrombosis. The minimally invasive procedures ensure effective relief with minimal downtime and discomfort.

Gynecology – Gynecological treatments at Pristyn Care cover a range of conditions including ovarian cysts, fibroids, endometriosis, and abnormal uterine bleeding. Pristyn Care also offers cosmetic gynecological procedures to enhance personal comfort and confidence, ensuring optimal outcomes with minimal recovery time.

Urology – Pristyn Care’s urology services include treatment for kidney stones, urinary incontinence, prostate enlargement, and erectile dysfunction. The urology team is also focussed on treating phimosis, paraphimosis, balanitis, and other penile health conditions, leveraging modern-age approaches such as laser circumcision and stapler circumcision.

Aesthetics – Pristyn Care offers aesthetic treatments such as liposuction, gynecomastia surgery, and cosmetic gynecology. These procedures are designed to improve physical appearance and boost self-confidence, using advanced and safe surgical methods.

Orthopedics –Orthopedic treatments at Pristyn Care include procedures for joint replacement, ACL reconstruction, and fracture repair. The use of cutting-edge technology and minimally invasive techniques ensures better outcomes and faster recovery.

Ophthalmology – Pristyn Care’s ophthalmology services address various eye conditions like cataracts, glaucoma, and refractive errors. Their eye specialists utilize the latest surgical techniques to provide clear vision and improve eye health.

Weight Loss – Pristyn Care provides weight loss solutions including bariatric surgery for individuals struggling with obesity. These procedures help in achieving significant weight loss, improving overall health, and reducing obesity-related complications.
