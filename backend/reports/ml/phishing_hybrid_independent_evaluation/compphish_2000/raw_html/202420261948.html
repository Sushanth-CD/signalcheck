    Underticketing & Dangerous Goods non-compliance fees

    Underticketing

    The following fees apply if the actual parcel weight or measurement, or the service requested for it, does not match the weight, measurement or service paid for by the purchased postage:

    • Standard Underticketing Fee: $5.50 excluding GST, plus unpaid postage
    • Courier Pack Underticketing Fee (for items incorrectly ticketed as Track Packs or Courier Packs instead of parcels): The difference between the correct size and the ticketed size, plus an administration fee of $5.50 excluding GST

    Dangerous Goods non-compliance

    If a parcel containing dangerous goods does not comply with our Dangerous Goods requirements, a non-compliance fee of $9.50 excluding GST will apply.

     

    * Delivery targets are a guide only. Delivery between some major towns and cities, and to and from rural or remote areas may take longer. Head to the delivery targets support page for more info.

    ** Maximum parcel size and weight: 25kg weight limit, 0.125m³ volume, 1.5m in length

    *** Please note Saturday delivery is not available for rural (RD) addresses.
