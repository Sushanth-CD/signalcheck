Time saving

Cut delays by eliminating old-fashioned credit card authorization forms and the unnecessary printing, faxing and manual keying of credit card details and payment postings.

PCI compliant

Payment data is encrypted in accordance with PCI-DSS 4.0.1 certification, to keep you PCI-compliant and at minimal risk when chargebacks occur.
