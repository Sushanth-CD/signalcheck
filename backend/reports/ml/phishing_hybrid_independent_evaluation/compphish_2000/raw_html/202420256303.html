LearnLive
MKCL’s LearnLive is a comprehensive software framework conceived, designed, developed and owned by MKCL for offering following services on Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) basis: Academic Lifecycle Management of Institute’s Programs, Courses, and Credits, Academic Lifecycle Management of Institute’s Colleges, Academic Lifecycle Management of Students and Academic Lifecycle Management of Teachers.
ExamLive
MKCL’ s most acclaimed framework for secured assessment and certification which provides turnkey solution for all assessment needs. Students, Teachers, Institutes and Corporates are best benefited by our products.
RecruitLive
Secure, Automated and Scalable platform to conduct end-to-end recruitment drive for any government and non-government organisations.
CampusLive
MKCL’s CampusLive is a comprehensive software framework conceived, designed, developed and owned by MKCL for offering various adminitstrative and acedemic services to Higher Educational Institutions on Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) basis.
TenderLive
TenderLive is a specialized B2B e-procurement platform designed for processing eTender of various sectors. It provides streamlined and efficient process for both tenderers and suppliers. It handles all tendering activities like preparing and publishing tenders, secured and transparent online submission and opening of bids with all supporting reports. With a large pool of registered suppliers, TenderLive makes online tendering process secure, smooth and cost-effective.
AuctionLive
AuctionLive is an innovative online platform designed to streamline the process of organizing and participating in auctions. It offers a comprehensive suite of features to manage every aspect of the auction lifecycle, from registration to final sale, ensuring a seamless experience for both organizers and bidders.

What is MKCL?
MKCL (Maharashtra Knowledge Corporation Limited) is a public limited company established to create new paradigms in Education, Development, Governance, and Empowerment (EDGE) through innovative digital solutions and capacity building.
What makes MKCL unique?
MKCL stands out by driving social transformation through technology, empowering individuals and communities. Its mission focuses on bridging the digital, knowledge, and development opportunity divides to create a more inclusive society.
Are the operations of MKCL limited to the State of Maharashtra?
No, MKCL's operations are not limited to Maharashtra. It has joint ventures and projects in Odisha, Haryana, Bihar, Kerala, and other states. Internationally, MKCL’s products are promoted in Saudi Arabia through its joint venture, MKCL Arabia Ltd.
Who are the members of MKCL’s Board of Directors?
The Board of Directors of MKCL typically consists of three nominees from the Government of Maharashtra, two Vice-Chancellors from the shareholding universities, six eminent experts from various sectors of knowledge, and the Managing Director, who serves as the whole-time director.
How can I enrol in MKCL’s educational programs?
You can explore and enrol in MKCL’s educational courses by visiting our Course section. There, you will find detailed information on available courses and the enrolment process.
