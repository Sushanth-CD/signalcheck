You’re venturing off-road in a car or four-wheel-drive vehicle. It’s important to be aware of the risks involved in remote travel. Imagine choosing a lonely trail, only to have your vehicle run into trouble. As you try to turn around and head back, your car might break down or get stuck. You could be miles from major highways, towns, or help, with no cell service. Even if your car is still running, you could face other issues like flat tires, and without the right tools to fix them, you might end up stranded.
